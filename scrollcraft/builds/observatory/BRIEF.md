# The civic observatory
Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. User requested two distinctly different award-level layered scroll sites for PolitikKu, with AIS as the main depth reference.

## Creative brief
1. Vibe: nocturnal, architectural, precise, spacious. Reference: AIS's independent depth planes; original composition and assets.
2. Journey: recognize Malaysia, understand the Seat, assemble the Dewan Rakyat, inspect the evidence, find your Seat.
3. Energy: an atmospheric opening, quiet explanation, one large spatial chamber assembly, restrained evidence, decisive lookup.
4. Feeling curve: recognition from skyline; curiosity from oversized type; clarity as 222 dots assemble; confidence from explicit sources; agency through working postcode lookup.
5. Signature: 222 individual seats move from an abstract constellation into a parliamentary hemicycle as the visitor scrolls. The threshold control highlights 112 dots without implying a current Coalition count.
6. Range: cinematic institutional design. Deep petrol, chartreuse, bone. Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Sans.
7. Structure: distinct scenes, custom observatory grammar. Minimal horizontal header; layered skyline hero; short text flow; pinned chamber; source ledger; search close. No video, repeated cards, or horizontal scroll traps.
8. Assets: local font files; generated photographic skyline cutout; repository postcode and Seat data. All imagery decorative. No invented statistics or live claims.

## Peak
It is the site where the whole country resolves into 222 Seats. The chamber sequence owns the largest scroll span.

# Learning Mission: The Election Model

**Goal:** Understand the current model used for election projections in the `live-political-analysis` repository, including its mechanics (the Swing Model) and the architectural decisions behind why it is used instead of a more complex predictive model.

**Context:** The user wants to understand how the system projects election outcomes (GE16) based on News Sentiment, Poll Calibration, and historical GE15 baselines, and why the "uniform state swing" approach was chosen with provisional constants (ADR 0001, ADR 0003, ADR 0005).
